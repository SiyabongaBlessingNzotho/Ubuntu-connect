TITLE: Ubuntu-Connect: AI-Powered Student At-Risk Detection

SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM
----------------------
• Struggling students are usually identified too late (after final exams)
• Attendance & grade data already exists but isn't analysed predictively
• Staff need proactive, explainable early-warning system

SECTION 2: THE SOLUTION
-----------------------
An AI system that scores every student's risk weekly and explains why, so staff can act early.

SECTION 3: ARCHITECTURE DIAGRAM
-------------------------------
[Weekly CSV Data] → [Feature Engineering: G1/G2 time-series] → [ML/DL Models: Random Forest + Keras] → [Risk API: FastAPI] → [Dashboard + Chatbot]

SECTION 4: KEY FEATURES
-----------------------
• Weekly risk scoring (0–1)
• Explainable reason codes for every flag
• Time-series trend detection (G1 → G2 → G3)
• Deep learning model (Keras neural network)
• Sentiment analysis of teacher notes (TextBlob)
• Chatbot for staff queries
